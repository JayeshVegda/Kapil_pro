Why we must separate Bills and Payments

Exact calculation logic
A. Item amount

Amount = Qty * Rate

B. Bags

For each item row:

Bags = Qty / 50

You can later round or allow manual correction if some bag is not exactly 50 kg.

C. Bill item total

For each bill:

Current Items Total = Sum of all item amounts for that bill

D. Total qty

For each bill:

Total Qty = Sum of all item qty rows

E. Total bags

For each bill:

Total Bags = Sum of all item bags

F. Grand bill amount

For each bill:

Grand Bill Amount = Current Items Total + Transport

G. Previous balance

For each new bill of a customer:

Previous Balance = Last closing balance of same customer

If no previous bill exists:

Previous Balance = Opening Balance from Customers sheet

H. Payment adjusted

This needs one strict rule.

There are 2 possible methods:

Method 1: Separate payment voucher system
payments entered in Payments sheet
bill print shows payments made since last bill or relevant payment entries
ledger always remains accurate
Method 2: Direct bill-wise adjustment
every bill manually selects which payments to show/deduct

For Excel, Method 1 is better.

So:

Closing Balance = Previous Balance + Grand Bill Amount - Payments Considered
