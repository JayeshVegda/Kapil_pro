# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Kapil Trading operators and administrators who create bills, record payments, inspect ledger balances, manage stock, and export reports during daily business work. They need fast access to common actions on desktop and phone, with readable data and minimal navigation friction.

## Product Purpose
Kapil Billing is a production billing and ledger workspace for sale bills, collections, customers, items, stock control, reports, print flows, and operational maintenance. Success means users can enter and review business records accurately, find information quickly, and trust financial status without visual or workflow ambiguity.

## Brand Personality
Practical, steady, and work-focused. The interface should feel like a dependable business tool: calm surfaces, clear hierarchy, direct labels, and restrained use of the existing blue and slate theme.

## Anti-references
Avoid marketing-page treatment, decorative dashboard chrome, generic card mosaics, heavy gradients, loud animation, and mobile layouts that simply squeeze desktop tables and controls into a narrow viewport.

## Design Principles
- Preserve desktop behavior and visual identity unless a task explicitly targets desktop.
- Make the highest-frequency actions reachable first on compact screens.
- Keep financial data readable with clear labels, adequate contrast, and stable spacing.
- Prefer familiar product UI patterns over decorative invention.
- Use motion only for navigation, disclosure, and state feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target practical WCAG AA behavior: readable text contrast, visible focus states, touch targets suitable for phone use, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and layouts that remain usable at narrow widths.
